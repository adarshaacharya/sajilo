# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People in Nepal and Nepali families abroad who need a fast, trustworthy desktop
companion for local dates, prices, public information, and everyday tasks.

## Product Purpose

Sajilo puts Nepal-specific daily essentials in the menu bar, with offline-first
calendar tools and clear freshness states for remote information. Success means
answering a small practical question quickly, in Nepali or English, without
making the user hunt across unfamiliar websites.

## Brand Personality

Local, calm, dependable. Sajilo should feel compact and familiar, with useful
information presented plainly and without noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic global dashboards, ad-heavy portals, noisy news feeds, hidden
network dependencies, and interfaces that imply uncertain information is live.

## Design Principles

- Make the most useful Nepal-specific action reachable in one or two clicks.
- Prefer offline and graceful degradation for essential information.
- Show freshness, source, and uncertainty explicitly.
- Use Nepali language and Bikram Sambat as first-class product concepts.
- Keep the tray companion compact, legible, and respectful of attention.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support Nepali and English, Devanagari and Latin numerals, keyboard focus, clear
contrast, large enough tap targets, and reduced-motion preferences.
