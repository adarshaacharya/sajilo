The **Bazar** tab has several sections along the top: **Stocks**, **Forex**, **Gold & Silver**, **Fuel** and **Vegetables**. Stocks opens first. Forex has [its own page](/docs/forex.html).

## Gold & Silver

- **Fine gold per tola** in large type, with the change since the previous price, the price per gram and a small price history.
- **Fine gold**, **Tejabi gold** and **Silver**, per tola and per 10 g.
- A **calculator** at the bottom: enter a quantity in tola, 10 g, grams or ounces, choose the metal, and see what it's worth.

Prices come from the **Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver Dealers' Association**.

## Fuel

**Petrol**, **Diesel** and **Kerosene** per litre and an **LPG cylinder**, with the change from the previous price and the date the prices took effect, from **Nepal Oil Corporation**.

## Vegetables

Today's **Kalimati** wholesale prices: the average, and the lowest and highest, per kg, per dozen or each.

- **Search** in Nepali or English.
- Hover over an item and click the **pin** to keep it at the top under **Pinned**.

> These are **wholesale** rates at Kalimati; shop prices are usually higher.

## Stocks: NEPSE

- **The index card:** the NEPSE index and its change, market turnover, whether the market is **Open** or **Closed**, a chart of today, and how many shares **rose**, stayed **unchanged** and **fell**. While the market is open, it updates every minute.
- **Search** for any listed company by name or symbol, and click it for its page: price, day and 52-week ranges, and trading figures, with a link to ShareSansar.
- **Movers:** the top **Gainers**, **Losers**, and the most traded by **Turnover** and **Volume**.
- **Sectors:** how each sector index moved.

### Follow companies

Click the **star** on a company to follow it. Followed companies appear in your **Watchlist** on the Stocks section. You can follow up to **12** companies.

### Track your shares

On a company's page, under **Your position**:

1. Click **Add purchase** and enter the **Quantity**, **Price per share** (click the market price to use it), the **Transaction date** and where you got the shares (secondary market, IPO, bonus…).
2. Sajilo estimates broker commission, SEBON fee and DP charge; open **Charges and note** to enter the actual charges from your contract note instead.
3. Click **Save transaction**.

When you sell, click **Record sale**; Sajilo estimates the capital gains tax and your realised profit or loss. Your watchlist becomes **Holdings**, with what you **invested**, today's **market value**, and **profit or loss**. Click a transaction to edit it, or the trash icon to delete it.

> Charges and tax are **estimates**. Your broker's contract note and MeroShare are final. Your transactions are stored **only on this computer**.

## IPOs

The **IPOs** card shows issues that are **open** or opening soon: when each closes, how many times it's **subscribed**, and a bar for the application window. **All ›** lists open, upcoming and recently closed issues.

Open an issue to see the details, then:

- **Apply on MeroShare** opens MeroShare in your browser.
- **I've applied** marks it, so Sajilo doesn't [remind you](/docs/reminders.html) on its closing day. Click **Undo** if you marked it by mistake.

## Upcoming dividends

Book closures that are coming up, with the bonus and cash dividend, and companies you follow first. Click one to open the company.

## Mutual funds and SIP

Switch **Stocks** to **Mutual Funds** at the top. Funds are grouped as **Open-ended (SIP)**, **Closed-ended** and **Matured**, with each fund's latest **NAV**. A NAV that hasn't been updated for a while says how many days old it is.

- **Star** a fund to keep it under **Your funds**.
- Enter **Your units** on a fund's page to see what they're worth, and how that changed this week. Units stay on this computer.

### SIP reminders

For an open-ended fund you pay into monthly:

1. Open the fund and turn on **SIP payment reminder**.
2. Choose the day it **Pays on**, an optional **Amount**, and when to **Remind me**: 7, 3 or 1 day before, and on the day.

When a payment is due, **Your funds** shows it with **Paid** and **Remind tomorrow**. SIPs also appear in [Keeper](/docs/keeper.html).

## Freshness

Prices refresh in the background every hour, and the market more often while it's open. Click **refresh** at the top for the latest. Without internet, Sajilo shows the last figures it has, marked **Showing older data**.

Bazar can be switched off in **Settings › Modules › Bazar**. This hides the whole tab, the NEPSE card on Today, and IPO and SIP reminders.
