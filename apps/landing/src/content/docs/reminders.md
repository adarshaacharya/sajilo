Sajilo can remind you about holidays, festivals, IPOs, SIP payments, your day plans and Keeper renewals. Everything is scheduled **on your computer**; nothing is sent from a server.

> **Sajilo has to be running** for reminders to arrive. It can be closed to the tray, and it starts with your computer by default. A reminder that came due while the computer was off or asleep still arrives if it's **less than 6 hours late**.

## Card or notification

Choose in **Settings › System › Reminders › How reminders appear**:

- **Card (recommended):** a small card at the top of the screen with a soft chime. It stays until you deal with it and never takes over your keyboard. Click **Show me an example** to see one.
- **Notification:** a normal system notification in the corner of the screen. Easier to miss.

On a card, **Open** takes you to the right place in Sajilo and **Got it** dismisses it. If several reminders arrive together, they wait in the same card (*2 more*). You can drag the card anywhere; it opens there next time.

## What Sajilo reminds you about

| Reminder | Turn it on or off | When it arrives |
|---|---|---|
| **Public holiday tomorrow** | Settings › System › Reminders | 7 pm Nepal time, the evening before |
| **Festival tomorrow** | Settings › System › Reminders | 7 pm Nepal time, the evening before |
| **IPO closing day** | Settings › System › Reminders | 10 am Nepal time on the closing day |
| **SIP payment due** | Settings › System › Reminders, and each fund's own switch in Bazar | 9 am Nepal time, on the days you chose |
| **Day plans** | The **Reminder** on each plan | At the plan's time, or the minutes before you chose |
| **Keeper** | **Remind me before** on each item | 9 am on each day you chose, and once the day after it's due |

The first four are **on by default**. The IPO and SIP reminders need the **Bazar** module on, and Keeper reminders need **Keeper** on: switching a module off in **Settings › Modules** silences its reminders too.

### IPO reminders

You won't be reminded about an issue you've marked **I've applied** in Bazar, or one that isn't open to the general public. See [Bazar](/docs/bazar.html#ipos).

### SIP reminders

Set these up per fund in **Bazar › Mutual Funds**: open a fund, turn on **SIP payment reminder**, then set **Pays on**, **Amount** (optional) and **Remind me**. See [Bazar](/docs/bazar.html#mutual-funds-and-sip).

## Notifications not showing?

If you chose **Notification** and nothing appears, notifications may be turned off for Sajilo in your system settings (on a Mac: System Settings › Notifications). **Do Not Disturb** and **Focus** modes also hide them. The **Card** style isn't affected by either. More in [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting.html#reminders-dont-arrive).

Break reminders from **Routine** have their own settings; see [Routine](/docs/routine.html).
