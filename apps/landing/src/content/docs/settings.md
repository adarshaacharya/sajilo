Open **Settings** with the **gear** at the top right of Sajilo, or from the tray icon's right-click menu (**Settings…**) on Windows and macOS. Settings has four tabs.

## Display

### Appearance

- **Theme:** System, Light or Dark.
- **Text size:** Small, Default or Large.
- **Language:** नेपाली or English.
- **Numerals:** Devanagari (२०८३) or Latin (2083).

### Menu bar

How the date looks in your menu bar or tray:

- **Format:** Short, Full (the default), With flag, Gregorian, Numeric, or **Custom…**, where you choose whether to show the Nepal flag and the BS year.
- **Show time:** adds the time in Nepal.

On Windows the tray shows the Nepal flag, and the date appears when you hover over it.

### Startup

- **Launch at login:** start Sajilo with your computer. It's on after installing; once you turn it off, it stays off.
- **Show Dock icon** (macOS only): show Sajilo in the Dock as well.

## Modules

Switch parts of Sajilo on or off: **Weather**, **Forex**, **News**, **Bazar**, **Rashifal**, **Radio**, **Second Clock**, **Keeper** and **Routine**. A switched-off module disappears from the tabs and the Today screen, and stops its reminders and background updates.

Some modules have their own options here:

- **Weather › Home place:** the place shown on Today, from the places you've pinned on the Weather screen.
- **Forex › Currencies on the card:** your favourite currencies.

## System

### Updates

**Check for updates**, and install one when it's ready. See [Updating Sajilo](/docs/updating.html).

### Reminders

How reminders appear (**Card** or **Notification**), and switches for **Public holiday tomorrow**, **Festival tomorrow**, **IPO closing day** and **SIP payment due**. See [Reminders](/docs/reminders.html).

### Privacy

**Help improve Sajilo** sends one anonymous count a day: the app version (and the one before it), your operating system and processor type, and a random number that isn't tied to you. It never includes your dates, notes or what you look at. Turn it off here.

### Backup

- **Export Sajilo data…** saves one file with your day plans, Keeper (including photos) and settings.
- **Import Sajilo data…** restores that file, for example on a new computer.

> **Importing replaces all of Sajilo's data on this computer** with what's in the file. News, prices and weather aren't in backups; they're fetched again.

## About

Your **version** of Sajilo, links to report a problem or get in touch, **Buy me a momo** if you'd like to [support Sajilo](/support.html), and the privacy policy. On Windows, **Can't find the tray icon?** shows the tip for pinning the flag to the taskbar again.
