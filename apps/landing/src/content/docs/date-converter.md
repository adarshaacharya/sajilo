The converter is in **Tools › Date Converter**.

## Convert a date

1. Choose the direction: **BS → AD** (Nepali to English) or **AD → BS** (English to Nepali).
2. Type the **year**, **month** and **day** as numbers. For Nepali months, Baisakh is 1 and Chaitra is 12.
3. The answer appears as you type.

The result shows the English date with its weekday, and the Nepali date with the month name.

- **Today** fills in today's date.
- **Swap** takes the answer, puts it into the fields and flips the direction, handy for checking.

## Copy the result

Under **Copy as**, click a line to copy it:

- **Nepali numerals:** the date as year/month/day in your numeral style;
- **English numerals:** year/month/day in 0–9 digits;
- **Long date:** the English date written out.

## Limits

Sajilo converts dates from **BS 1992 to 2090** (about AD 1935 to 2034). A date outside that range, or one that doesn't exist, shows a red message explaining why. The converter works offline.

> Any day in the calendar also has **copy buttons** on its page, if you just need today's date. See [The Nepali calendar](/docs/calendar.html#a-days-page).
