The official **Nepal Rastra Bank** rates are in **Bazar › Forex**.

## Convert money

At the top is a converter:

1. Type an **amount**.
2. Pick a **currency**.
3. Click **swap** to change direction.

Foreign currency → rupees uses NRB's **buying** rate; rupees → foreign currency uses the **selling** rate. The line under the result says which rate was used, because banks and money changers work the same way.

## The rates

- **Favourites** come first, then **All currencies**.
- Each row shows the **buying** rate, with **sell** underneath.
- Some currencies are quoted **per 100** or **per 10** units, like the Japanese yen; the row says so.

At the bottom: when NRB **published** the rates, and a link to nrb.org.np. On days NRB doesn't publish, the latest rates still in force are shown.

## Choose your favourites

Favourites are set in **Settings › Modules › Forex › Currencies on the card**. The converter starts on your first favourite.

Forex can be switched off in **Settings › Modules › Forex**; the tab in Bazar then disappears.
