Sajilo runs on Macs with **Apple Silicon** (M1 and newer) and on **Intel** Macs. Pick the matching download: if you're not sure, open  → **About This Mac** and look at **Chip** or **Processor**.

Beta builds are not signed with an Apple certificate yet, so macOS asks you to confirm the app the first time. You do this once per update.

## Install

1. Download Sajilo for your Mac, then open the `.dmg`:

   [Mac with Apple chip (M1 or newer)](/dl/macos-arm64) [Intel Mac](/dl/macos-x64)
2. Drag **Sajilo** onto the **Applications** shortcut.

![Dragging Sajilo onto the Applications shortcut](/assets/install/0-drag-to-applications.png)

## Allow it the first time

3. Open Sajilo from Applications, Launchpad or Spotlight. macOS says **“Sajilo” Not Opened**. Click **Done**, not Move to Trash.

![The “Sajilo” Not Opened dialog](/assets/install/1-not-opened.png)

4. Open **System Settings** → **Privacy & Security** and scroll down to **Security**. Next to *“Sajilo” was blocked to protect your Mac*, click **Open Anyway**.

![The Security section with the Open Anyway button](/assets/install/2-open-anyway.png)

5. Confirm **Open Anyway** once more, then use Touch ID or your password.

![The confirmation dialog](/assets/install/3-confirm-open.png)

## When it's installed

Sajilo lives in the **menu bar** at the top of the screen, showing today's Nepali date. Click the date to open it.

It starts with your Mac from now on; you can turn that off in **Settings › Display › Startup › Launch at login**. Sajilo has no Dock icon by default, because it's a menu-bar app; you can show one with **Settings › Display › Startup › Show Dock icon**.
