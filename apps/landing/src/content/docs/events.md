To see what's coming, go to the **Today** tab and click **All events ›** on the **Up next** card.

## The list

It shows the named festivals and holidays from today onward, up to a year or so ahead. Each row has:

- the name, **in red if it's a public holiday**;
- when it is: *Today*, *Tomorrow* or *In 12 days*;
- the date in both calendars.

Click a row to open that day's page, with its tithi, panchang and your day plan.

## Filters

At the top, choose what to see:

- **Current:** the next seven days.
- **Festivals:** every named festival and holiday. This is the default.
- **Public holidays:** holidays only.

The list comes from the calendar built into Sajilo, so it works offline. It covers festivals up to **BS 2083**; later years will be added as they're published.
