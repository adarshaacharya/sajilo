**Today** is the first tab and the screen Sajilo opens on. Everything on it is a shortcut: click a part to go deeper.

## The date at the top

- **The date tile** shows today's Bikram Sambat day and weekday. Click it to open [today's full page](/docs/calendar.html#a-days-page): tithi, festivals, sunrise, Rahu Kaal and the panchang.
- Next to it: the Nepali month and year, today's date in English, and a clock.
- **The clock always shows Nepal time**, even if your computer is set to another country, so it's useful from abroad.
- At the right: **Settings** (gear) and **Quit** (power). When an update is ready, an update button appears here too; see [Updating Sajilo](/docs/updating.html).

## Notices

Occasionally a short notice from Sajilo appears under the date, for example an IPO window or a fix you should know about. Click it to open its link. Close it with **✕** and it won't come back. Urgent notices can't be closed; they disappear on their own when they expire.

## World clocks

If you've added cities in **Tools › Second Clock**, their times show here as small chips, with a **+1** or **−1** when that city is already on another day. Drag a chip to reorder; hover over it and click **✕** to remove it. See [Tools](/docs/tools.html#second-clock).

## The calendar

This month in Bikram Sambat, with the English dates underneath. Holidays are red, and a small dot marks days where you've added a plan. Click any day to open it. [More about the calendar →](/docs/calendar.html)

## Up next

A short list of what's coming:

- the next festival and the next **public holiday**;
- an **IPO** that's open now (if Bazar is on); issues you've marked as applied are left out;
- **Keeper** items due within a week;
- a **SIP** payment due within three days.

Anything urgent, like an IPO closing today or a payment due, moves to the top in gold. Click a row to open it, or **All events ›** for the [full list of upcoming festivals](/docs/events.html).

## At a glance

- **Routine**: when your next break is, and how much water you've had, if you use [Routine](/docs/routine.html).
- **Weather**: your home place's temperature, high and low. Click for the [full forecast](/docs/weather.html).
- **NEPSE index**: today's value and change. Click for [Bazar › Stocks](/docs/bazar.html). It updates live while the market is open.

Under the cards, **Updated … ago** tells you how fresh the weather and market figures are.
