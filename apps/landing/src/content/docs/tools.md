The **Tools** tab has everyday helpers in two groups. Click one to open it, and the back arrow to return. Results update as you type; in the calculators, **click any result to copy it**.

## Everyday

### Directory

Phone numbers and websites you might need, in one place.

- Switch between **Phone numbers** and **Websites & apps**.
- Filter phone numbers by **Emergency**, **Government**, **Health** and **Utilities**; websites by **Checkers**, **Portals** and **Apps**.
- **Search** for a service or number.
- Click a number to call it (with a calling app), or a website to open it.

### Second Clock

Keep an eye on family and colleagues abroad.

1. Search for a **city or region**.
2. Click it to add it. Added cities show their local time, and **+1** or **−1** when they're on another day.

Your cities also appear on the **Today** screen under the date. Click **✕** to remove one.

### Date Converter

Nepali ↔ English dates. See [Date converter](/docs/date-converter.html).

## Calculators

### Land

Enter an area and pick its unit: **Ropani**, **Aana**, **Paisa**, **Daam**, **Bigha**, **Kattha**, **Dhur**, square feet or square metres. Sajilo shows it in:

- the **hill system**: ropani–aana–paisa–daam;
- the **Terai system**: bigha–kattha–dhur;
- square feet and square metres.

1 ropani = 16 aana = 5,476 sq ft. 1 bigha = 20 kattha = 72,900 sq ft; 1 kattha = 20 dhur.

### Weight

Convert between **tola**, **grams**, **10 g** and **troy ounces**, as used for gold and silver. 1 tola = 11.6638 g.

### VAT

Enter an amount and see the price **before VAT**, the **VAT** and the **total**, at Nepal's 13%. Turn on **Price already includes VAT** to take VAT out of a price instead of adding it.

### Interest

Enter the **principal**, the **rate % per year** and the number of **years** (quarters are allowed) to see the **simple interest** and the **total**.
