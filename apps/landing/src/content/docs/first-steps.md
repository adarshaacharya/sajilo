Sajilo is a small window that opens from your **menu bar** (Mac), **system tray** (Windows) or **top bar** (Linux). It doesn't sit on your taskbar or in the Dock; it waits in the tray and opens when you want it.

## Opening and closing it

| | Open Sajilo | Close it |
|---|---|---|
| **Windows** | Click the Nepal flag next to the clock | Click the flag again, or click anywhere else |
| **macOS** | Click the Nepali date in the menu bar | Click the date again, or click anywhere else |
| **Linux** | Click the date in the top bar, then **Open Sajilo** | Choose **Hide Sajilo** from the same menu |

On every system, **Esc** closes it too. Closing only hides the window: Sajilo keeps running, so the radio keeps playing and reminders still arrive.

On Windows and macOS, **right-click** the tray icon for a short menu with the date, **Settings…** and **Quit Sajilo**.

> **Opening it from the Start menu, Launchpad or your applications menu** also works, any time. If Sajilo is already running, its window just comes to the front.

On Linux you can also bind a key to `sajilo-desktop --toggle`, which opens and closes Sajilo; see [tiling window managers](/docs/install-linux.html#on-a-tiling-window-manager).

## The tabs

Along the bottom of the window:

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Today** | The date, the Nepali calendar, what's coming up, weather and NEPSE at a glance |
| **News** | Headlines from Nepali and English newsrooms, and government notices |
| **Bazar** | Gold and silver, fuel, Kalimati vegetables, NEPSE, IPOs and mutual funds |
| **Rashifal** | Today's horoscope for your sign |
| **Radio** | Nepali FM stations |
| **Tools** | The date converter, Nepali units, world clocks and more |
| **Routine** | Break reminders and your screen time |
| **Keeper** | Documents and renewals, like a passport, licence or insurance |

**Today** and **Tools** are always there. The others can be switched off in **Settings › Modules** if you don't use them; a switched-off tab disappears and stops sending reminders.

## Settings and quitting

On the **Today** screen, the icons at the top right are **Settings** (the gear) and **Quit** (the power button). Quit closes Sajilo completely, until you open it again or restart the computer.

## Starting with your computer

Sajilo turns on **Launch at login** the first time it runs, so the date is there every day. Started at login, it waits quietly in the tray. To turn this off: **Settings › Display › Startup › Launch at login**. Once you turn it off, it stays off.
