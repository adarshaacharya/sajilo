A **day plan** is anything you want to remember on a date: a meeting, a bill, a birthday, a puja. Plans are kept on your computer only.

## Add a plan

1. On the **Today** tab, click the day in the calendar.
2. Scroll to **Day plan** at the bottom of the day's page and click **+ Add plan**.
3. Type what you need to do.
4. Optionally:
   - turn on **Add a time** and pick the **Time**;
   - choose a **Reminder**: *At time*, or 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or 1 hour before;
   - choose **Repeat**: *One-time*, *Every BS month* or *Every BS year*;
   - add a **short note**.
5. Click **Save plan**.

The day now shows a small dot in the calendar.

> A **reminder needs a time**. Plans without a time are notes for the day, with no reminder.

## Repeating plans

Repeats follow the **Nepali calendar**. *Every BS year* suits birthdays and anniversaries kept by the Nepali date, and *Every BS month* suits monthly bills. If a monthly plan is on a day some months don't have, like the 32nd, it falls on the last day of shorter months.

## Change or delete a plan

- **Change it:** click the plan, edit it, and click **Save plan**. A repeating plan keeps its original start date.
- **Delete it:** hover over the plan and click the **trash icon**.

> **Deleting happens straight away, with no confirmation.**

## When the reminder arrives

The reminder arrives at the time you chose, as a card at the top of your screen or a system notification, depending on **Settings › System › Reminders**. See [Reminders](/docs/reminders.html). Sajilo needs to be running (it can be closed to the tray) for reminders to arrive.
