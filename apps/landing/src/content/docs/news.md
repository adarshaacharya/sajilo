The **News** tab gathers the latest headlines from newsrooms across Nepal into a single list, newest first.

## The sources

| Group | Newsrooms |
|---|---|
| **Official** | Nepal Government |
| **Nepali** | OnlineKhabar, Kantipur, Annapurna Post, Ratopati, Gorkhapatra, Bizkhabar, Artha Sansar, TechPana, Hamro Khelkud |
| **English** | OnlineKhabar English, The Kathmandu Post, Khabarhub, Ratopati English |

Headlines appear in the language each newsroom publishes in.

## Reading

- Each headline shows its **source** and how long ago it was published.
- **Click a headline** to open the full article in your browser.
- Scroll down to load more.

## One source at a time

Use the **Source** menu at the top to choose **All sources** or a single newsroom. Sajilo remembers your choice.

## Government notices

Headlines from **Nepal Government** open inside Sajilo, on an **Official update** page with:

- the department and the date and time it was published;
- the full text, which you can select and copy;
- any **Attachments**, like PDFs, which open in your browser;
- **Open government portal ↗** for the original.

## When a source is down

If a newsroom can't be reached, the others still show, with a note underneath: *Could not reach …*. When you're offline, Sajilo shows the last headlines it had. Click **refresh** at the top to try again.

News can be switched off in **Settings › Modules › News**.
