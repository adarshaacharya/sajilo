The **Rashifal** tab shows the **daily** reading for your sign.

## Choose your rashi

The first time, Sajilo shows **Choose your rashi**: all twelve signs, each with the syllables that belong to it.

Find the **syllable your name begins with** and click that sign. Your rashi comes from your name or your birth chart, **not your birth month**.

## Your reading

Your sign's name, its syllables and today's reading. Readings are in Nepali.

- To look at another sign, click it under **All signs**. **My rashi** takes you back.
- To change your own sign, click **Change**.

If the day's reading isn't out yet, Sajilo shows the latest one and tells you it was published for an earlier day.

## Every morning

Once you've chosen your sign, Sajilo asks once: **Your rashifal every morning?** Say **Yes, every morning** and today's reading arrives on its own, a few minutes after you **first sit down at your computer** each morning:

- It waits for **5 minutes of use** after 5 am, so it doesn't land while you're logging in, then for a pause in your typing.
- Once a day, and **never after noon**. If you've already read it in the Rashifal tab, it doesn't come.
- It only ever shows **today's** reading, never yesterday's.

Turn it on or off any time in **Settings › System › Reminders › Daily rashifal**.

Rashifal can be switched off in **Settings › Modules › Rashifal**, which silences the morning reading too.
