The **Rashifal** tab shows the **daily** reading for your sign.

## Choose your rashi

The first time, Sajilo shows **Choose your rashi**: all twelve signs, each with the syllables that belong to it.

Find the **syllable your name begins with** and click that sign. Your rashi comes from your name or your birth chart, **not your birth month**.

## Your reading

Your sign's name, its syllables and today's reading. Readings are in Nepali.

- To look at another sign, click it under **All signs**. **My rashi** takes you back.
- To change your own sign, click **Change**.

If the day's reading isn't out yet, Sajilo shows the latest one and tells you it was published for an earlier day.

Rashifal can be switched off in **Settings › Modules › Rashifal**.
