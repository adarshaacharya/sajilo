The calendar is on the **Today** tab. It works completely **offline**: every date, festival and holiday is built into Sajilo.

## Reading the month

- The heading shows the **Nepali month and year**, with the English months it spans.
- Use **‹** and **›** to go to the previous or next month. Click the **Today** tab to come back to this month.
- Each day shows the **Nepali date** large and the **English date** small underneath. Today is highlighted.
- **Red days are holidays**: every Saturday, Sundays under the government's Sunday holiday rule, and public holidays.
- A **small dot** means you've added a [day plan](/docs/day-plans.html) there.
- **Hover** over a day to see its festival, or its tithi.

Digits follow your setting in **Settings › Display › Numerals** (Devanagari or Latin).

## A day's page

Click any day to open its page. Use **‹** and **›** at the top to step to the previous or next day.

**The summary** shows:

- the date in both calendars, and how far away it is (*Today*, *Tomorrow*, *In 5 days*…);
- the **tithi**;
- the **festival**, marked **Public holiday** when it is one;
- **Saturday holiday** or **Sunday holiday** where they apply;
- **Saait** for weddings or bratabandha, on auspicious days;
- **copy buttons** for the date. Click one to copy the date exactly as shown: Nepali in Devanagari digits, Nepali in English digits, or the English date.

**Sun and time** shows the length of daylight, **sunrise** and **sunset**, **Rahu Kaal** (traditionally avoided for starting something new) and the **Chaughadiya** for day and night, with good, neutral and bad parts coloured. On today's page, the current part is highlighted.

**Panchang** lists the **tithi**, **nakshatra**, **yoga**, **karana**, the **moon** and its rashi, **moonrise and moonset**, the **season** and the **Nepal Sambat** date, each with the time it ends.

At the bottom is your **Day plan** for that day; see [Day plans](/docs/day-plans.html).

> Sun, Rahu Kaal and panchang times are worked out on your computer **for Kathmandu**, and shown in Nepal time.

## How far the calendar goes

- Dates can be converted from **BS 1992 to 2090** (about AD 1935 to 2034).
- **Festivals, tithi and public holidays are included for BS 2066 to 2083.** Outside those years, only Saturdays and Sundays are marked.
- Years **2085 to 2090** are marked *Provisional — not yet officially published*, because the government hasn't published them yet.
