**Routine** looks after you on long days at the computer: short breaks for your eyes and back, water, meals and bedtime, and a week of screen time.

> **Nothing about what you do is recorded.** Sajilo only asks your computer how long ago the keyboard or mouse was used. It never sees which apps you use or what you type.

## Turn it on

Open the **Routine** tab and click **Turn on**. You'll get:

- **Rest your eyes:** every 20 minutes, look at something about 6 metres away for 20 seconds;
- **Stand up:** every hour, walk for a couple of minutes;
- **Water:** every hour, drink a glass.

Sajilo then asks **When do you usually eat and sleep?**: set times for breakfast, lunch, dinner and bedtime, or click **Not now**.

Click **Show me an example** to see a break card before turning anything on.

## When reminders come

- **Only while you're using the computer.** Twenty minutes means twenty minutes at the screen. **Stepping away for 5 minutes counts as a break**, so the timers start over.
- **Watching a video or on a call counts too**, even without touching the keyboard.
- **Only during work hours** (9:00 to 18:00, Sunday to Friday, unless you change them) and **not on public holidays**.
- **Meals and bedtime** come at your times, work hours or not, but only if you're at the computer.

## The break card

A small card appears at the top of your screen (at the bottom on Windows), with a chime and a funny line. It never takes over your keyboard.

- **Done** when you've taken the break. For eyes and standing, a ring counts down, and the break counts as taken when it runs out.
- **+250 ml** on the water card logs a glass.
- **Remind in 5 min** puts it off for five more minutes of computer use.
- **Skip** dismisses it.

Drag the card anywhere; it opens there next time. If eyes and standing are both due, you get just the stand-up card, since standing rests your eyes too.

## The Routine screen

- **Next break:** what's coming and when, or why nothing is (paused, outside work hours, a holiday…).
- **Switches** for each reminder. The **Water** row has **+** and **−** to log glasses, and shows today's total against your goal.
- **Last 7 days:** your screen time each day (hover over a bar for the exact time), your daily average, **breaks taken**, days you reached your **water goal**, and your **longest time without a break**.
- **Pause for 1 hour** when you need to concentrate; **Resume** brings reminders back early.

## Settings

Click **Settings** on the Routine screen:

- **How often, how long:** the interval for each reminder, how long eye and stand-up breaks last, and your **daily water goal**.
- **Meals and bedtime:** times and switches.
- **Your own reminder:** name anything, like *Stretch your wrists*, and choose how often.
- **Work hours:** **From** and **To**, the **work days**, **Nudge me when work ends**, and **Quiet on public holidays**.
- **Remind with:** a **card** or a system **notification**. This is shared with the other [reminders](/docs/reminders.html).
- **Funny lines** and **Play a soft chime**.
- **Turn off break reminders** switches everything off, keeping your settings for next time.

Everything stays on your computer. Switching Routine off in **Settings › Modules** stops it measuring entirely.
