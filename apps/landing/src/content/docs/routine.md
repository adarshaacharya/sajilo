**Routine** looks after you on long days at the computer: short breaks for your eyes and back, water, meals, bedtime and a nudge to stop work, and your screen time for the day and the week.

> **Nothing about what you do is recorded.** Sajilo only asks your computer how long ago the keyboard or mouse was used. It never sees which apps you use or what you type.

## How it works

Three rules explain every number on the Routine screen:

- **Timers only run while you're using the computer.** "Every 20 min" means 20 minutes at the computer, not 20 minutes on the clock.
- **Stepping away counts.** Be away for a break's length (20 seconds for your eyes, 2 minutes to stand up) and it counts as a **break taken**, card or no card. Away for 5 minutes or more and the timers start over.
- **Breaks don't interrupt.** During a call, a fullscreen video or slides, or with Do Not Disturb on, breaks wait and come as soon as it's over.

The Routine screen shows these rules once under **How Routine works**; click **Got it** to put them away.

## Turn it on

Open the **Routine** tab and click **Turn on**. You'll get:

- **Look away:** every 20 minutes at the computer, look at something far away for 20 seconds;
- **Stand up:** every hour at the computer, walk for a couple of minutes;
- **Water:** a reminder if you haven't logged any water in an hour.

Sajilo then asks **When do you usually eat and sleep?**: set times for breakfast, lunch, dinner and bedtime, or click **Not now**.

Click **Show me an example** to see a break card before turning anything on.

## The Routine screen

From the top:

- **What's next:** the next break and how long until it, and the one after. When nothing is counting down it says why: paused, holding for a call, a day off. **Pause** stops reminders for **30 min**, **1 hour** or the **rest of the day**; **Resume** brings them back early.
- **Water:** tap the glass each time you drink; it adds 250 ml. The bar shows today's litres against your goal. **Undo** takes back the last tap.
- **While you work:** look away, stand up, water and **your own reminder** (name anything, like *Stretch your wrists*). Each row says how often it comes. **Click a row** to change how often, how long the break lasts, your **daily water goal** in litres, or to see its card.
- **At set times:** breakfast, lunch, dinner, bedtime and **Stop work**, a single card at your stop time on your work days (shown on the row). Click a row to change its time.
- **Today / This week:** your screen time, **breaks taken** out of those you were reminded of, and your **longest time without a break**. Switch to **This week** for a bar per day, your daily average and the days you reached your water goal.

## The break card

A small card appears at the top of your screen (at the bottom on Windows), with a chime and, if you like, a funny line. It never takes over your keyboard.

- **Done** when you've taken the break. For eyes and standing a ring counts down, and the break counts as taken when it runs out.
- **+250 ml** on the water card logs water.
- **Remind in 5 min** puts it off for five more minutes at the computer.
- **Skip** dismisses it.

Drag the card anywhere; it opens there next time. If eyes and standing are both due, you get just the stand-up card, since standing rests your eyes too.

## Routine settings

Click **Routine settings** at the bottom of the Routine screen:

- **Don't interrupt me when:** you're in a call, an app is fullscreen, or Do Not Disturb is on. Each can be switched off.
- **Days off:** your **work days**, and what happens on days off and public holidays: **Lighter** (eye breaks, water and your own reminder, but no stand-up and no stop-work nudge), **Same as work days**, or **No breaks** (meals and bedtime still come).
- **How reminders appear:** a **card** or a system **notification**. This applies to all of Sajilo's [reminders](/docs/reminders.html), not only breaks.
- **Funny lines** and **Play a soft chime**.
- **Turn off break reminders** switches everything off, keeping your settings for next time.

Everything stays on your computer. Switching Routine off in **Settings › Modules** stops it measuring entirely.
