Click the **weather card** on the **Today** tab to open the full weather screen.

## What you see

- **Now:** the temperature, the sky, how warm it **feels**, today's **high and low**, and the **chance of rain**.
- **Air quality:** the US AQI number and what it means, from *Good* to *Hazardous*, with one line of advice, and the **PM2.5** and **PM10** readings.
- **Tomorrow**, then the **next few days** with rain chance and highs and lows.

Temperatures are in °C.

## Choose places

Sajilo knows every **district headquarters** and the larger towns, in English and Nepali.

1. Click the **place name** at the top (or **Add**) to open **Places**.
2. **Search** for a town, district or province, in English or Nepali, or browse by province.
3. Click a place to see its weather. It's added to your **pinned places**, the row of chips under the forecast, so you can switch back with one click.

To remove a place, click the **✕** on its chip. You always keep at least one.

## Your home place

The **home place** is the one shown on the Today screen, marked with a house. To change it, open a pinned place and click **Show on home**. You can also pick from your pinned places in **Settings › Modules › Weather › Home place**.

## Freshness

The home place's weather is refreshed in the background every hour; other places update when you open them. Click the **refresh** arrow for the latest. If Sajilo can't reach the forecast, it shows the last one it has, marked **Showing older data**.

Weather can be switched off in **Settings › Modules › Weather**, which also removes the card from Today.
