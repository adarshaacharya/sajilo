**Keeper** remembers the dates you can't afford to miss: a passport or licence expiring, the bluebook tax, an insurance premium, the rent. **Everything stays on your computer.**

## Add something

Click **Add document** or **Add reminder** and pick what it is.

**Documents you hold:** Citizenship certificate, National ID, PAN, Passport, Driving licence, Bluebook, Insurance policy, Warranty, or **Other document** with your own fields.

**Things to do or pay:** Electricity, water, internet and telephone bills, rent, a loan EMI, school fees, a medicine refill, a subscription, or **Something else**.

Every form has a **For** choice: **Me**, someone you've already added, or **+ Someone new** to add a family member. You can then filter Keeper by person along the top.

## Documents

Each kind of document asks for what matters for it:

- **Kept on file** (citizenship, National ID, PAN): the number, issue date and office. These never expire, so they never remind you.
- **Expires** (passport, driving licence, warranty, travel insurance): the **expiry** date.
- **Repeats** (bluebook tax every year, vehicle and health insurance, life insurance premiums): the next **due** date.

Dates are picked on a **Nepali calendar**, and shown in both calendars.

**Remind me before** chooses when you're told: from a year before down to the day itself. A passport, for example, reminds you a year, six months and a month before it expires.

## Reminders for bills and tasks

Give it a name, an optional **Due date**, and a **Repeat**: one-time, every Nepali or English month, or every Nepali or English year. Choose **Remind me before**, then **Save reminder**. A reminder without a date is a note, with no notifications.

## Photos

Add photos of the document itself, up to 10 each: click **Add photos**, drag them in, or paste. Photos stay on your computer, inside Sajilo's data, so they're included in your [backups](/docs/settings.html#backup). Click a photo to open it larger, where you can zoom, rotate, save a copy or delete it.

## When something is due

**Coming up** at the top shows what's due soon: documents from two months ahead, reminders from two weeks.

- **Paid a bill?** Click the tick. A repeating reminder moves on to its next date (*Mark paid — moves to the next date*); a one-time one is marked done.
- **Renewed a document?** Open it and use **Mark tax paid**, **Mark renewed** or **Mark premium paid**, or **Renewed…** to enter the new expiry date.

Keeper also warns you about a bluebook with no linked vehicle insurance, or insurance that ends before the tax is due.

## Reminders

Keeper reminds you at **9 am** on each day you chose, and once more the day after something becomes overdue, as a card or a notification. See [Reminders](/docs/reminders.html).

SIP payments set up in [Bazar](/docs/bazar.html#sip-reminders) also appear here.

Keeper can be switched off in **Settings › Modules › Keeper**, which also stops its reminders.
