Sajilo starts dark and gold, but most of how it looks is yours to change, in **Settings › Display** (the **gear** at the top right).

## Light or dark

**Theme** follows your computer by default (**System**). Choose **Light** or **Dark** to keep one whatever the computer does.

## Accent colour

The accent is the colour of buttons, today's date on the calendar, the pin and other highlights. Pick a dot under **Accent colour**; point at one to see its name first. Every colour is made to read well in light and dark.

## Text size and language

- **Text size:** Small, Default or Large, for the whole app.
- **Language:** नेपाली or English. **Numerals:** Devanagari (२०८३) or Latin (2083).

## Mini view

Want the date on your desktop without the whole window? Press **Mini view** (the two inward arrows, next to the pin at the top right). Sajilo shrinks to a small strip:

- **First line:** today in BS, the date in AD, and the time in Nepal.
- **Second line:** your choice. **Click it** to switch between the next festival or holiday, the weather, the NEPSE index, the dollar rate, and the radio station while one is playing. The same choice is in **Settings › Display › Mini view**.
- A running [stopwatch](/docs/tools.html#stopwatch) takes the second line while it has time on it; click to pause.

The strip stays open when you click elsewhere and can be **dragged anywhere**. It's remembered, strip and position, after a restart. Press the **outward arrows** on the strip to open the full view again; unpin it and Sajilo is the normal popover once more.

## Keep it open

Press the **pin** at the top right and Sajilo stays open while you work, drags by its top bar, and reopens where you left it. **Esc** or the tray icon still puts it away. See [First steps](/docs/first-steps.html#keeping-it-open).

## The menu bar date

How the date looks in your menu bar or tray (short, full, with the flag, with the time, or your own mix) is under **Settings › Display › Menu bar**. See [Settings](/docs/settings.html#menu-bar).
