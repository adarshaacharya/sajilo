# Third-party attributions

Sajilo is built for Nepal, and built in Nepal. Almost everything it shows — news, weather, forex, gold and silver, fuel, vegetable prices, rashifal, festival dates — is somebody else's data, gathered from public sources and put in one place. None of it is ours.

**Sajilo does not resell any of this data.** It is free, carries no
advertising, and no subscription sits behind any of it.

**Sajilo is open source.** The code that fetches, parses, and displays every source below is in this repository, for anyone to read, audit, or fork.

## Sources

Sajilo sources its data from:

- **News:** OnlineKhabar, Annapurna Post, Ratopati, Bizkhabar, OnlineKhabar English, The Kathmandu Post, Khabarhub, Ratopati English, and Gorkhapatra, via each publisher's own RSS feed; Kantipur, via the keyless public JSON endpoint its own front end reads.
- **Rashifal:** Hamro Patro
- **Exchange rates:** Nepal Rastra Bank, the country's central bank.
- **Gold and silver rates:** the Federation of Nepal Gold and Silver
Dealers' Association (FENEGOSIDA).
- **Fuel prices:** Nepal Oil Corporation.
- **Vegetable prices:** the Kalimati Fruits and Vegetable Market
Development Board.
- **Weather and air quality:** [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com), licensed [CC BY 4.0](https://open-meteo.com/en/license).
